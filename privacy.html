Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

TrendRunner operates primarily as a client-side application. Core market data is fetched directly in your browser. An optional free account (email/password or social login via Supabase Auth) is available so your watchlist and Holdings paper-tracker can sync across devices.

2. Local Storage

We use your browser's Local Storage to remember watchlist, locked Holdings (including paper entry price and lock time), UI preferences, and short-lived market caches. This data stays on your device unless you sign in.

3. Optional Account Sync

If you create an account and sign in, TrendRunner stores your watchlist, locked coin IDs, and Holdings paper-meta in your Supabase Auth user metadata so they can be restored on another device. We do not store exchange API keys, balances, or trade execution credentials.

4. API Usage and Third-Party Services

In order to provide real-time market data, your browser makes direct requests to third-party public APIs (such as Binance, DefiLlama, and Alternative.me). Authentication is handled by Supabase. These services may collect standard web analytics data, such as your IP address, according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Affiliate Tracking

Some links on our platform, specifically trading links leading to cryptocurrency exchanges, are affiliate links. If you click these links, the destination exchange may place a tracking cookie on your device to attribute any subsequent account creation or trading activity to TrendRunner. This does not result in any additional cost to you.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
