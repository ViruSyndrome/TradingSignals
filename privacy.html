Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

TrendRunner operates primarily as a client-side application. Core market data is fetched directly in your browser. An optional free account (email/password or social login via Supabase Auth) is available so your Watch list and Holdings paper-tracker can sync across devices.

2. Local Storage

We use your browser's Local Storage to remember:

  • Watch list (⭐ ideas you starred)
  • Holdings locks (🔒) including paper entry lots — price, date/time, optional quantity
  • Suggestions you marked as followed (personal win/loss ledger)
  • UI preferences, alert/outcome research journals, and short-lived market caches

This data stays on your device unless you sign in. It is for paper tracking and research — we do not place trades on your behalf.

3. Optional Account Sync

If you create an account and sign in, TrendRunner stores your Watch list, locked coin IDs, Holdings paper-meta (including multi-lot entries), and followed-suggestions ledger in a durable Supabase portfolio table when available (with auth metadata as fallback) so they can be restored on another device. We do not store exchange API keys, balances, or trade execution credentials.

4. API Usage and Third-Party Services

In order to provide real-time market data and logos, your browser may request third-party public APIs (such as Binance, DefiLlama, Alternative.me, and public icon CDNs). Authentication is handled by Supabase. These services may collect standard web analytics data, such as your IP address, according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Affiliate Tracking

Some links on our platform, specifically trading and registration links leading to cryptocurrency exchanges, are affiliate / referral links (for example Binance ref=TRENDRUNNER). If you click these links, the destination exchange may place a tracking cookie on your device to attribute any subsequent account creation or trading activity to TrendRunner. New Binance accounts created via our referral may receive a fee kickback from Binance. This does not result in any additional cost to you versus using Binance directly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
