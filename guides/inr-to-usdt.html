INR → USDT for trading

Goal: turn bank INR into USDT you can use on Spot (BTCUSDT, etc.), then follow TrendRunner High Conf / stop plans.

Recommended flow

  1. Create or open Binance via a TrendRunner referral link (new accounts may get 10% fee kickback).
  2. Complete KYC if prompted — P2P limits rise when verified.
  3. Use P2P Buy USDT with UPI / IMPS / e-Rupee (not Convert — see why).
  4. After USDT arrives, transfer from Funding → Spot if needed.
  5. Open the pair from a TrendRunner card (“Trade on Binance”) and place your entry + OCO / 50/50 exits.

Checklist before you buy a signal

  • Start with High Conf on the dashboard — not every BUY is a Strong Buy.
  • Know stop, bank TP, and runner trail before you click buy.
  • Risk only 1–2% of trading capital per idea.
  • Lock the coin in Holdings after fill so paper PnL matches your Binance price.
