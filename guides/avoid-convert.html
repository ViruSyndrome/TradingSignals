Why not use Convert (or similar instant routes)

Convert on Binance (and similar “instant buy/sell” buttons elsewhere) is built for speed. Convenience often hides a worse all-in rate than P2P.

TrendRunner’s stance for India: use P2P for INR ↔ USDT when you care about rate. Use Convert only for tiny amounts when you knowingly accept the spread.

What you lose

  • Hidden spread: The quoted Convert price embeds a margin vs mid-market. On large sizes that is real money.
  • Less transparency: P2P shows competing merchant prices; Convert shows one bundled offer.
  • Habit risk: Repeated Convert on every deposit/withdrawal compounds fee drag on your edge.
  • Wrong tool for cash-out: Dumping USDT via Convert to a wrapped bank product (when available) can be worse than selling USDT on P2P to your own UPI/IMPS.

When Convert is OK

  • Very small dust balances.
  • You compared the Convert quote to a top P2P ask/bid and the difference is tiny.
  • You need speed and accept paying for it.
