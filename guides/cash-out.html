Cash out: USDT → INR → bank

After you bank profits on Spot, move trading currency (USDT) back to your bank via P2P Sell — not Convert — for a clearer rate.

Steps

  1. Sell crypto to USDT on Spot (or leave profits in USDT).
  2. Transfer USDT to the wallet Binance P2P uses (often Funding) if the app asks.
  3. Open P2P → Sell → USDT → INR.
  4. Choose a yellow-tick buyer with strong completion rate; prefer UPI/IMPS you already use.
  5. Place the sell order → wait for the buyer’s INR payment → confirm the money in your bank app → then release USDT in escrow.
Never release USDT because someone sent a screenshot. Check the actual bank / UPI credit first. If payment is wrong or delayed, use Binance appeal — do not chat off-app.

Timing & costs

  • P2P platform fee is typically 0%; the rate includes the merchant’s spread.
  • Bank / UPI credit is usually minutes; weekends and bank maintenance can delay.
  • Large sells: split into smaller orders with reputable merchants.
  • Keep records for your own taxes — TrendRunner does not provide tax advice.

Do not

  • Use Convert as your default cash-out (why).
  • Accept “I’ll pay from a friend’s account” mismatches that break escrow rules.
  • Withdraw to unknown third-party wallets for “better rates.”
