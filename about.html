About TrendRunner

TrendRunner is now a BTC Red Bar Desk: a visual map of prior-day range bands, today’s opening range, and red-bar exit references, with a clear LONG / SHORT / WAIT strip. It is educational research — not financial advice, and it does not execute trades for you.

What you see on the desk

  • Predicted trend from opening-range break + interaction with prior-day bands
  • Chart — BTC 30m price, EMA 10/30 overlay, OR / band / stop / exit lines
  • Action strip — what bias to take and where stop / book references sit

Two separate ideas

  • Red Bar / ranges — trend prediction (the core)
  • EMA 10/30 — separate overlay (agree / disagree only)

Spot vs futures

Spot is long-only. To profit from a SHORT bias you need futures (or margin). Leverage adds liquidation risk — start small, isolated margin, always use a stop.

Funding (India)

P2P guides are only for moving INR ↔ USDT into your wallet — not the strategy itself: P2P funding, avoid Convert, INR → USDT, cash out.

Legacy

The old multi-coin High Conf / moonshot / Strong Buy scoreboard is hidden. Code may still exist for research; the live product path is Red Bar on BTC.

Philosophy

Transparent levels on a chart beat a black-box “buy this alt” list. You remain responsible for every order on the exchange.
