About TrendRunner

TrendRunner is an advanced, algorithm-driven trading dashboard designed specifically for the cryptocurrency market. Our mission is to cut through the noise of social media hype and emotional trading by providing pure, data-driven technical analysis.

How It Works

Our proprietary engine calculates a composite score for every asset in real-time. We analyze independent technical and fundamental factors, including:

  • Dual Moving Average Crossovers (EMA 9/21 & SMA 50/200)
  • Momentum (RSI)
  • Trend Strength (MACD)
  • Volatility and Squeeze Metrics (Bollinger Bands)
  • Fundamental TVL (Total Value Locked) via DefiLlama
  • Global Market Sentiment (Fear & Greed Index)

Our Philosophy

We believe in transparent, mechanical trading. Every "BUY" and "SELL" signal generated by TrendRunner is the result of strict mathematical conditions. We do not use "black box" AI or subjective analysis. What you see is exactly what the data is doing.

The Moonshot Scanner

In addition to our core blue-chip and altcoin tracking, TrendRunner features a dedicated Moonshot Scanner that hunts for micro-cap assets experiencing extreme volume anomalies and volatility squeezes on the 4-hour timeframes, allowing you to catch breakouts before they hit the mainstream.
