About TrendRunner

TrendRunner is an algorithm-driven trading dashboard for cryptocurrency markets. Our mission is to cut through social hype and emotional trading with transparent, data-driven technical analysis. Signals are educational research — not financial advice, and TrendRunner does not execute trades for you.

How It Works

A shared composite engine scores each asset in real time. Core factors include:

  • Dual Moving Average Crossovers (EMA 9/21 & SMA 50/200)
  • Momentum (RSI)
  • Trend Strength (MACD)
  • Volatility and Squeeze Metrics (Bollinger Bands)
  • Fundamental TVL (Total Value Locked) via DefiLlama (where available)
  • Global Market Sentiment (Fear & Greed Index)
  • Entry timing chips: CONFIRM vs WAIT (daily 4H dips, moonshot retests, 5m scalp retests)
  • Heuristic boosters (experimental): SMC liquidity sweep, Wyckoff spring, volume-profile POC — pattern detectors, not a claim of live “whale tracking”

Three Engines

  • Daily core — validated winner lists, hard regime/greed gates, strict Strong Buy rules (score ≥ 4, 100% confidence, cool RSI).
  • Moonshot scanner (4H) — breakout setups with retest timing; high risk. Results stay in the Moonshots tab unless you Watch or Lock them.
  • 5m Scalps — short EMA-pullback plays on liquid volatile alts; small size only.

Strong Buy Rule

Trusted Strong Buy badges require agreement across directional factors plus a cool RSI. Heuristic SMC / Wyckoff / POC points can raise a score toward Buy, but they cannot mint a trusted Strong Buy alone.

Holdings & Suggestions

Lock 🔒 after you buy to paper-track positions. You can correct the exact Binance fill price and date/time, and add multiple entry lots. Tap I followed this on a Buy signal to log suggestions you took and track wins vs losses in Signal Log. Signed-in users can sync Watch, Holdings, and followed ledger via Supabase.

Exits

Live playbook is a 50/50 plan: bank half near +10%, trail the rest, with a 7-day max hold on leftovers. Always place protective stops on the exchange.

Our Philosophy

We believe in transparent, mechanical trading. Every BUY and SELL is the result of stated mathematical conditions — not a black-box AI. Historical backtests refresh weekly for the daily core list; moonshot, scalp, and SMC ablation runs are research-only and do not rewrite live allowlists by themselves.
