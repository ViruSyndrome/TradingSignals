About TrendRunner

TrendRunner is a Red Bar Futures Desk for BTC, XAU (Gold), and NIFTY: a visual map of mark-window Fibonacci boxes (0 · 0.44 · 0.5 · 0.56 · 1), the 1H first-red daily pivot (Trade 1 departure & Trade 2 revisit), and the Asia → London → US session liquidity clock, complete with a Futures LONG / SHORT / WAIT action strip and isolated-margin sizing. It is educational research — not financial advice, and it does not execute trades for you.

The 4 Pillars on the Desk

  • 1. Mark-Window Fib Boxes (0 / 0.44 / 0.5 / 0.56 / 1): Drawn from each market’s institutional mark window (BTC 02:30–03:30 IST, XAU 01:30–02:30 IST, NIFTY 15:15–15:45 IST) and extended right across the session. Inside [0, 1] is sideways accumulation (WAIT); outside is directional expansion.
  • 2. 1H First Red Candle (Session Pivot): After skipping the opening candle of the day, the first closed 1H red candle marks the daily pivot. Trade 1 rides the morning departure away from that bar; Trade 2 watches the 2–3h revisit (reject = continue trend; reverse = flip direction).
  • 3. Session Liquidity Clock: Pre-London holds the morning bias; London (12:30 IST) sweeps Asia High/Low and reverses; US Open (16:30–18:30 IST) hunts London H/L (and untouched Asia H/L).
  • 4. Structural Stops, +0.8R Breakeven & ≥1.5R Targets: Stops sit at recent local swings or the 1H red pivot (never arbitrarily inside the Fib box). At +0.8R, move the stop to Breakeven (+0.10% fee cover) and book at the nearest liquidity magnet (Prior-Day H/L, London/Asia H/L, or Fib extension) offering ≥1.5R.

Futures Execution & Risk Sizing

The desk is built for Futures LONG and SHORT execution. Both the inline Futures Sizing bar on the desk and the Risk Calculator tab compute your 1R dollar risk, position notional, contract quantity, recommended safe isolated leverage, and estimated liquidation price so your structural stop always triggers well before liquidation.
