About TrendRunner

TrendRunner is an algorithm-driven trading dashboard for cryptocurrency markets. Our mission is to cut through social hype and emotional trading with transparent, data-driven technical analysis.

How It Works

A shared composite engine scores each asset in real time. Factors include:

  • Dual Moving Average Crossovers (EMA 9/21 & SMA 50/200)
  • Momentum (RSI)
  • Trend Strength (MACD)
  • Volatility and Squeeze Metrics (Bollinger Bands)
  • Fundamental TVL (Total Value Locked) via DefiLlama (where available)
  • Global Market Sentiment (Fear & Greed Index)
  • Entry timing chips: CONFIRM vs WAIT (daily 4H dips, moonshot retests, 5m scalp retests)

Three Engines

  • Daily core — validated winner lists, hard regime/greed gates, strict Strong Buy rules.
  • Moonshot scanner (4H) — breakout setups with retest timing; high risk. Results stay in the Moonshots tab unless you Watch or Lock them.
  • 5m Scalps — short EMA-pullback plays on liquid volatile alts; small size only.

Holdings & Suggestions

Lock 🔒 after you buy to paper-track positions. You can correct the exact Binance fill price and date/time, and add multiple entry lots. Tap I followed this on a Buy signal to log suggestions you took and track wins vs losses in Signal Log.

Exits

Live playbook is a 50/50 plan: bank half near +10%, trail the rest, with a 7-day max hold on leftovers. Always place protective stops on the exchange — TrendRunner does not execute trades.

Our Philosophy

We believe in transparent, mechanical trading. Every BUY and SELL is the result of stated mathematical conditions — not a black-box AI. Historical backtests refresh weekly for the daily core list; moonshot and scalp backtests are research-only.
