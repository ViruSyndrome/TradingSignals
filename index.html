📚 Learn — TrendRunner Desk

Plain-English guide to session structure: leave, retest, sweep, mid-zone reject, afternoon window. Telegram alerts when ENTER is ready.

⚖️

1. Mid-zone & mark-window levels

Each session can mark an open candle and draw levels 0, 0.44, 0.5, 0.56, 1 on that structure:

  • Mid-zone (44–56%): The middle band of the open mark candle — where price often retests after the first leave.
  • Mid-zone reject (ENTER): After the open candle is marked (NIFTY ~09:15; BTC Asia Trikal ~05:30), wait for leave → retest of mid-zone → SHORT only if a 5m candle rejects back below mid. Close through the top of mid-zone = idea invalid.
  • Also ENTER paths: breakout leave, shelf retest, liquidity sweep, afternoon window. Stretch snapback stays a chart guide only.
  • Mark windows: BTC ~US cash close · NIFTY late-session window · used as targets / context, not automatic entries.
  • Inside the full [0, 1] box: often chop → WAIT.
🕯️

2. Session box — leave & retest

Mark the session’s first opposing (red) candle as a box: high, low, and middle. Then drop to 5 minutes:

  • Breakout leave (ENTER): Price closes clearly outside the box — first expansion.
  • Shelf retest (ENTER): After a leave, price returns to the box edge; a 5m candle must close back through that edge in trend direction. A wick alone is not enough.
  • Liquidity sweep (ENTER): Highs/lows get hunted, then price closes back through — follow the confirmed direction.
💡 Keep Alerts ON — you should not stare at the desk. Telegram fires when ENTER is ready.
🧲

3. Stretch snapback (chart guide)

When price runs far from a short average on the 30-minute chart for several bars, it often snaps back:

  • Desk may draw a “barricade” and a mean target as a WATCH / overlay.
  • This is not a default Telegram ENTER — use it to avoid chasing extremes.
🕒

4. Afternoon window (NIFTY ~12:45 IST)

After the morning range and lunch quiet zone:

  • Morning range: Lock high / mid / low for context.
  • Lunch quiet: Prefer no new entries until the afternoon window.
  • Afternoon reversal: Trap buyers/sellers inside the early-afternoon candle, then reclaim.
  • Afternoon breakout: Clean expansion beyond morning high or low — or a shelf retest if you missed the first print.
🎯

5. Risk ladder — stop, bank, trail

  • 1️⃣ Stop = structure: Beyond a recent 5m swing (or mid-zone edge). That distance is 1R. No ENTER until the stop is real.
  • 2️⃣ Bank ~50% at 1:1 → move stop to cost on the runner.
  • 3️⃣ Trail toward 1:2 / 1:3 or marked session highs/lows. Fib lines on the desk are mostly targets.
⚡

6. Futures sizing & safe leverage

Crypto desk paths are built for perpetual futures (LONG and SHORT). NIFTY alerts can map to options on your broker:

  • Prefer isolated margin so one wick cannot wipe the whole wallet.
  • Let stop distance set size — risk a small % of capital, keep leverage modest.
  • Use the desk sizing bar or the Risk tab before placing an order.
📈

7. Moving averages & session levels

  • After banking at 1:1, many traders trail the runner with a short / medium average cross.
  • Desk marks Asia / London / New York (or NSE) highs & lows plus prior-day levels.
  • Averages are helpers — they are not the ENTER trigger by themselves.

📖 TrendRunner Desk Guide & Rules

🎯 Breakout leave

Price leaves the session box (first opposing candle high/low) with a clear close-through. ENTER with a 5m structural stop · bank ~50% at 1:1 → move stop to cost → trail the runner.

⚡ Shelf retest

After a leave, price returns to the box edge. A 5m candle must close back through that edge — a wick alone is not enough. Stop on the 5m swing · 1:1 → 1:2 → 1:3 ladder.

⚖️ Mid-zone reject

Open candle marked → leave → retest mid-zone (44–56%) → SHORT reject. Fill near mid · stop beyond top of mid-zone · bank at session anchor / 2R. Live on BTC sessions and NIFTY open.

🧲 Stretch snapback (guide)

When price stretches far from a short 30m average for several bars, it often snaps back. Desk may draw a barricade + mean target. Chart guide only — not a default Telegram ENTER.

🕒 Afternoon window (~12:45 IST)

After lunch quiet, mark the early-afternoon candle. Reversal = trap that auction then reclaim. Breakout = expansion beyond morning high/low. Live ENTER when triggered.

💡 FAQs & Rules of Execution

What is the difference between WAIT and WATCH?
• WAIT = Market is in equilibrium or consolidation between boundaries. No directional bias or trade setup is present. Stay in cash.
• WATCH = Directional bias or structure is identified (session box forming, average alignment, or liquidity sweep underway), but the exact entry trigger has NOT fired yet. Prepare your order, but DO NOT enter prematurely.

How do Futures Fees affect small vs large sizes?
Binance USD-M Futures fee is 0.04% per side (0.08% round-trip). Because fee is a fixed percentage, trading a $3,000 position for a small move makes 3× more net dollars than a $1,000 position for the same price movement. On losing trades, the fee remains 0.08% of notional.

Set-and-Forget Two-Leg Execution:
Every ENTER signal provides identical Entry and Stop Loss. Leg A books 50% profit at 1:1 risk-to-reward and immediately moves Stop Loss to Breakeven. Leg B is a risk-free runner targeting 1:3 or average rollover.
