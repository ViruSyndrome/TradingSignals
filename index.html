📚 Learn — Red Bar Desk

How to read the BTC chart, LONG / SHORT / WAIT, and place your own trade

🟥

What TrendRunner is now

The old multi-coin High Conf / moonshot scoreboard is retired from the main UI. The live product is the Red Bar Desk: one BTC 30m map with trend bias and levels. It does not place orders for you.

  • Primary: Prior-day range bands + today’s opening range + last red-bar refs → BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL.
  • Action strip: LONG, SHORT, or WAIT with entry lean, stop, and exit/book levels.
  • Chart: Price, EMA 10/30 (separate overlay), opening range, band magnets, stop & exit lines.
  • You: Confirm on the chart → trade on the exchange (futures for shorts; spot only for longs).
📐

Red Bar theory (the gold)

This is the trend prediction layer — not the same thing as EMA crosses.

  • Every prior day gets a high / mid / low band, extended forward. No cherry-picking days.
  • First candle of the day on 30m = opening range. Do not trade it. After it locks: break above high → bullish bias; break below low → bearish; stuck inside → WAIT.
  • Price back inside an old band often means pullback / balance — watch for hold or rejection.
  • Last red bar high/low = book-profit and structure references (as in the course-style demos).
💡 Same geometry can later apply to Nifty, gold, oil, gas — once that market’s OHLC is wired. Live desk = BTC first.
📈

EMA overlay (separate theory)

EMA 10 / 30 on the chart is a second opinion, not the Red Bar engine.

  • Desk shows whether EMA agrees or disagrees with the Red Bar trend.
  • If they disagree, size smaller or skip — do not force the trade.
↕️

LONG, SHORT, WAIT — what to do

  • LONG — Bias up. Futures: open long. Spot: buy only if you accept long-only. Stop at invalidation; book near exit line.
  • SHORT — Bias down. Needs futures / margin (spot cannot short-first). Stop above invalidation; book near exit line.
  • WAIT — Inside opening range or no clear break. Mark the yellow OR lines and do nothing until a hold outside the box.
💡 Always place stop + take-profit on the exchange when you enter. The desk shows references; the exchange enforces them.
⚡

Futures basics (for SHORT and leverage)

  • Long = profit if price rises. Short = profit if price falls.
  • You post margin; leverage multiplies gains and losses. Wrong side → liquidation risk.
  • Start with low leverage (2×–3×), isolated margin, tiny size, and a hard stop every time.
  • Transfer USDT to the futures wallet only after you understand funding fees and liquidation.
💱

🛡️

Risk rules

  • Risk only 1–2% of trading capital per idea (smaller with leverage).
  • No stop = no trade.
  • Never average a losing futures position.
  • If the desk flips to WAIT or the opposite bias, exit and reassess.
💡 Use the Risk sidebar calculator for position size before you click buy/sell on the exchange.
