📚 Learn — Red Bar Futures Desk

Mark-window Fibs · H1→M5 Trade 1 / Trade 2 · ENTER only with 5m swing SL · Telegram on ENTER · Futures sizing

🟥

1. Mark-Window Fib Boxes (0 · 0.44 · 0.5 · 0.56 · 1)

Each session starts with an institutional mark window drawn forward across the chart with custom Fibonacci levels 0, 0.44, 0.5, 0.56, 1:

  • BTC: 02:30–03:30 IST (1H wick H/L) · XAU (Gold): 01:30–02:30 IST · NIFTY: 15:15–15:45 IST institutional close.
  • Inside [0, 1]: Sideways accumulation zone → WAIT (do not force trades inside the box).
  • 0.44–0.56 Golden Pocket: Institutional equilibrium zone on pullbacks into prior-day or current-day Fibs.
  • Outside [0, 1]: Directional expansion (above Fib 0 = Bullish; below Fib 1 = Bearish).
🕯️

2. The 1H First Red Pivot (Trade 1 & Trade 2)

On the 1H chart, mark the first closed 1H red candle of the IST day — HIGH/LOW (liquidity) + median + Fib 44/50/56. Desk ladder is H1 → M5:

  • Trade 1 (Departure): Price leaves the 1H first-red bar — bias in that direction.
  • Trade 2 (Revisit): Sweep of 1H high or low liquidity, then a 5m close back through that level (median confluence).
  • ENTER gate: Telegram / action strip only go ENTER when the stop locks to a 5m swing (quality filter from public USD-M outcomes).
💡 Keep Alerts ON — you should not stare at the desk. Worker + open-tab relay push Telegram on ENTER (~1–2×/day on average).
🕒

3. Session Liquidity Clock (Asia → London → US)

Institutions hunt session highs and lows rather than random mid-air targets:

  • Pre-London (until 12:30 IST): BTC typically keeps its post-mark morning direction.
  • London Open (12:30–16:30 IST): Watch for a liquidity sweep of Asia High or Low followed by a reversal.
  • US Open (16:30–18:30 IST): Heavy institutional flow hunts London H/L (and untouched Asia H/L). Drop to the 1-minute chart when it zooms and ride the impulse.
🎯

4. 5m Swing Stop · 1:1 → BE → 1:2 → 1:3

  • 1️⃣ Stop = 5m swing: Beginner SL sits beyond the recent 5m swing low (LONG) or 5m swing high (SHORT). That distance is 1R. No ENTER (and no Telegram) until this stop is locked.
  • 2️⃣ 50% at 1:1 → BE: Bank half at +1R, move stop to breakeven on the runner.
  • 3️⃣ Trail to 1:2, target 1:3: After 1:2, trail; book remaining into 1:3 or marked liquidity (Asia / London / NY / prior day H/L). Mark-window Fibs are targets only.
⚡

5. Futures-Only Execution & Safe Leverage

The Red Bar Desk is built for USDⓈ-M Perpetual Futures (both LONG and SHORT):

  • Always use Isolated Margin so a black-swan wick cannot drain your entire wallet.
  • Let your stop distance dictate your leverage: if your stop is 1.0% away and you risk 1% of capital, your position notional equals 1× your wallet (use 2×–5× isolated leverage so liquidation stays far beyond your stop).
  • Use the inline Futures Sizing bar on the desk or the Risk calculator tab before placing any order.
📈

6. EMA 10 / 30 Runner Trail & Marked Session Levels

EMA 10 / 30 serves both as directional alignment at entry and as the exit trigger for your 50% runner after 1:1 hits:

  • Runner Exit: After banking 50% at 1:1, hold the remaining 50% as long as EMA 10 stays stacked with your trade; close when EMA 10 crosses EMA 30.
  • Always Mark Key Liquidity: The desk marks Asia, London & New York Session H/L, Previous Day H/L, and the 5m swing used for the stop.
  • EMA is overlay only on this desk — not the Red Bar ENTER trigger.
